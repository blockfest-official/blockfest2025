About BlockFest

BlockFest is a youth-led community festival celebrating creativity, culture, and modest fashion. It’s a space where young people come together to showcase talent, share ideas, and build community.

Founded by teens for teens, BlockFest blends streetwear, music, art, and empowerment into one unforgettable day.
